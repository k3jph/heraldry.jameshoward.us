# Howard voice review

Reviewed 10 October 2026 against the Howard voice skill, its revision protocol,
reasoning grammar, and calibration examples, and ten supplied James P. Howard, II
writing samples. The samples informed prose and argument structure; their subject
matter was not treated as factual authority for this site.

## Scope and editorial result

The review covered public page copy, shared navigation and footer copy, all 38
long-form chapters, the 12 guided lecture texts, and all 35 object studies. It also
covered reference descriptions, the 399 lexicon definitions, translation-analysis
notes, workshop prompts, and the privacy and cookie explanations. This was a
voice and content-structure review, not an independent specialist verification of
every source or translation.

| Corpus | Coverage | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public pages | 31 Astro page templates, including shared chapter and object templates | Revised generic introductions, promotional fragments, repeated defensive phrasing, and mismatched heading/body transitions. Kept useful labels and operational instructions concise. |

## Reasoning used in the revision

The paragraph is the principal unit of explanation. A useful paragraph introduces
something the reader can inspect, establishes the relationship needed to explain
it, considers the complication, and derives a consequence. The sequence can span
several paragraphs where the subject requires it. It is not a fixed sentence
count or a repeated template.

| Section | Working object or model | Argument and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage and about | A shield moving between media | Recognizable design leads to questions about the bearer, the object, and the source supporting the association. Tools and readings serve those connected questions. |
| Anatomy and charges | One bird in three positions; an inventory of figures | Position and relationship determine heraldic role. Knowing the noun alone cannot reconstruct the display. |
| Tinctures and geometry | A fixed star with changing field; a division compared with an ordinary | Hold one feature constant and observe what changes. The result explains a layer or coordinate relationship before invoking a convention. |
| Blazon | One description given to different artists | Required relationships survive changes in style; explicit count or placement changes require different wording. |
| Cadency and marshalling | Related bearers; repeated components in a composite | Describe the observed difference or arrangement before establishing its familial function through documentary evidence. |
| Jurisdiction and regional chapters | An application, statute, register, or manuscript | Identify actors, acts, versions, and source relationships. A familiar English term cannot establish an unfamiliar institution's effect. |
| Object studies | Armour, textile, coin, vessel, leaf, or dated chapel view | The material and its use determine which observations and historical claims are available. A missing connection becomes a defined research question. |
| Transcription and collation | Located lettering and separate editorial layers | Keep the observed form, normalization, and translation retraceable. Agreement produced by editing or copying cannot independently confirm the source used to produce it. |
| Capstones | A bounded question and a witness inventory | Select evidence capable of answering the question; show the intermediate steps and identify a source that could change the conclusion. |

Specific changes include:

- The regional essays now begin more often with the actual documentary problem,
  allowing their comparison with other systems to follow from the source.
- Object interpretations develop the mechanism behind their cautions. A textile's
  replaced arms, for example, lead to an investigation of continued use and changed
  ownership rather than a disconnected reminder that dates can be uncertain.
- Repeated arms on the armour ensemble are described as repeated uses of a design,
  not seven independent confirmations of a bearer. That corrects a source-dependence
  inference without changing the catalogue attribution or visible count.
- Headings and surrounding prose now agree about the question under discussion.
  The homepage's surname paragraph, for example, sits under a bearer-attribution
  heading rather than a heading about the crest's position.
- Humor was retained where it arises from the situation. Prose does not reproduce
  the author's casual chat manner or invent personal experiences.

## Preservation and validation

A pre-edit snapshot protects 1,981 source, rights, translation, assessment, and
witness fields. Comparison with the revised files confirms that these fields are
unchanged. The check also confirms unchanged footnote definitions, block
quotations, and Markdown link targets in all 38 chapters. Institutional titles,
object dates and identifiers, alternative attributions, image licences and
credits, original excerpts, working translations, and quiz answers were retained.

Existing automated tests cover source and image integrity, object comparisons,
transcription and collation, geometry, exports, consent behavior, and curriculum
structure. Publication validation also includes Astro diagnostics, a complete
static build, and live checks of representative revised pages. These checks
establish implementation integrity; the specialist-review statuses on the site
remain in place.
