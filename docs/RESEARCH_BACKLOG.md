## Stage B — substantive continental survey

- [ ] France: medieval armorials, blasonnement, d'Hozier, the 1696 fiscal registration, Revolutionary rupture, public symbolism.
- [ ] German-speaking lands: early `Wappenbücher`, Bürgerwappen, family arms, civic arms, modern voluntary `Wappenrollen`, Swiss cantonal divergences.
- [ ] Iberia: Spanish regional differences, cronistas, royal/municipal armory; Portuguese `Livro do Armeiro-Mor`, `Livro da Nobreza e Perfeiçam das Armas`, colonial afterlives.
- [ ] Low Countries: Dutch nobility and public-body records; Flanders' explicit private-arms decree; Wallonia and Luxembourg differences.
- [ ] Italy: medieval civic heraldry, republican cities and papal context, the former Consulta araldica, Italian constitutional settlement, archival evidence.
- [ ] Poland–Lithuania: `herb`, `ród herbowy`, `zawołanie`, clan-bearing patterns, Union of Horodło, revision of antiquarian `herbarze`.
- [ ] Nordic: Sweden's Riksarkivet and public register; Denmark, Norway, Finland, Iceland, and the relationship to law and graphic identity.
- [ ] Czechia/Slovakia/Hungary: municipal and aristocratic arms, national archives, the Czech REKOS register, genealogical case studies.
- [ ] Balkans and Eastern Europe: source-limited contexts, state emblems, Ottoman and Habsburg administrative overlays.

## Coverage and review

Completion criteria apply **per topic**, not to the entire site. A profile marked `source-ledger` is reconnaissance, `synthesized` is an original English research note, and `reviewed` requires human specialist review. The number of covered regions is not a substitute for depth.

Continuous goal: eliminate gaps in this backlog, add newly discovered source traditions, and revisit contested scholarship. A checkmark is not a stopping signal.
